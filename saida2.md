## Evaluation Notes

The survey is broad in scope and well-structured, covering NMR fundamentals, petrophysical applications, EOR monitoring, unconventional reservoirs, and advanced techniques. However, it often reads more as a comprehensive compilation of findings than as a critical synthesis, and it is undermined by editorial inconsistencies, including mixed citation styles and an unresolved meta-comment in the permeability section. The main strengths are its topical breadth and logical progression; the main weaknesses are limited comparative analysis and insufficient editorial control.

### Coverage — 4

The survey covers most foundational and emerging areas, including relaxation mechanisms, pulse sequences, petrophysics, EOR, unconventional reservoirs, multi-dimensional NMR, high-field NMR, and MRI. However, coverage is uneven: Logging While Drilling (LWD) is emphasized in the abstract and conclusion but lacks a dedicated section, and subsections on tight gas sands and heavy oil/oil sands are comparatively thin. Some figures rely on non-petroleum examples, which weakens selective domain focus.

### Relevance — 4

Content is strongly aligned with the stated NMR-in-oil-and-gas scope, and even advanced topics are generally tied back to reservoir characterization or EOR. Occasional digressions, such as the sea cucumber CPMG example, yeast-cell high-field NMR spectra, and groundwater/glacier SNMR imaging, are only partially integrated into the petroleum context. These are isolated rather than systemic, so the overall relevance remains high.

### Structure — 4

The organization is clear and progressively layered: fundamentals, petrophysical applications, EOR monitoring, unconventional reservoirs, and advanced techniques. Transitions are mostly logical, but some subsections—especially within advanced MRI and surface NMR—feel abrupt or only loosely connected to prior petroleum-focused sections. Within several subsections, content is presented as a sequence of observations rather than developed conceptual groups.

### Synthesis — 3

The survey groups prior work by application area and includes some useful comparisons, such as NMR permeability models and pore-size distribution integration with MICP. However, there is limited analytical synthesis: many paragraphs describe individual studies sequentially without critical comparison or extraction of general principles. The absence of comparative tables and the uneven use of conceptual frameworks reduce the interpretive depth below the level expected of a strong review.

### Consistency — 2

There are frequent editorial inconsistencies. Citation style alternates between numeric references and author-year parenthetical citations, sometimes within the same section. Figure captions mix styles and formats. Most seriously, Section 3.3 contains an unresolved meta-comment discussing which SDR model form is being used, explicitly noting an inconsistency in the manuscript itself. Terminology and equation presentation are mostly stable, but these lapses indicate insufficient final editing.
